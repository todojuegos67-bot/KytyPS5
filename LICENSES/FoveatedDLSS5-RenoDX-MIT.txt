DLSS 5 Neural Rendering on Vulkan, foveated (src/local/dlss-nr.cpp, src/local/dlss-nr.comp; KYTY_DLSS_NR,
KYTY_FOVEATED, the launcher's "DLSS foveado").

Ported from FoveatedDLSS5 (https://github.com/maximedgoulet/FoveatedDLSS5): its foveated NR working grid
(src/addons/dlss5/FOVEATION.md, foveation.hpp, shaders/foveation.hlsli, shaders/fov_guides.cs_5_1.hlsl), rewritten
from HLSL / D3D12 to GLSL / Vulkan; and the DLSS Neural Rendering feature contract of the RenoDX DLSS 5 add-on it is
built on (https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx, src/addons/dlss5: dlssnr.hpp, bridge_params.hpp): the feature id,
the DLSSNR.* parameters, the host-owned parameter block and the runtime's caller-identity import patch.
Both are distributed under the MIT License below (FoveatedDLSS5 retains RenoDX's notice).

No NVIDIA software comes with this package: nvngx_dlssnr.dll is NVIDIA's and must be supplied by the user.
